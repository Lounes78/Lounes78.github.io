About me

Hi! I’m Lounes. I’m a deep learning engineer working on speech, multimodal AI, and the systems used to train and run models at scale.

I studied electronics and computer science at the National Polytechnic School of Algiers, then at Paris-Saclay University, where I graduated top of my class. I’m now finishing a master’s degree in Intelligent Systems Engineering at Sorbonne University.

From September 2024 to February 2026, I worked at Ampere / Renault Group on TTS, speech-to-speech, and real-time inference. I’m currently completing my end-of-study research internship at IFM, MBZUAI, focused on multilingual ASR and large-scale GPU workloads.
